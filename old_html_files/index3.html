What this can't see

Grip type and grip force

Pose estimation gives a wrist, not fingers. Crimp vs sloper vs pinch, and how hard each is being pulled, are invisible here — and they are most of what decides whether a move goes. This is the same signal missing from robot manipulation datasets: video records that contact happened, never what it cost.
